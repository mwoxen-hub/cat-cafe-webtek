Adopt a cat

Why adopt?

By adopting a cat, you help save lives by reducing shelter overcrowding, and you give a cat a loving home. Spending time with cats can also help lower stress and blood pressure.

Not all of our cats are up for adoption. Some of them live permanently at the café. You can see which cats are looking for a new home on our Cats page.

How it works

  1. Fill out the adoption form below.
  2. We will call you to set up a meeting at the café.
  3. At the meeting, you get to know the cat, and we make sure it is a good match for both of you.
  4. If everything feels right, you can take your new friend home.

Adoption fees

  • Kittens (under 1 year): 2,000 kr
  • Adult cats (1–8 years): 1,500 kr
  • Senior cats (over 8 years): 1,000 kr

All cats are ID-marked, neutered and fully vaccinated before they move to their new home.
