Client

Our client is Cathrine Cat. She is the owner of Purr & Pour, a cat cafe dedicated to animal rights and mental health.

Email: cathrine@purrandpour.com

Phone: +47 270 19 367

From our last meeting October 2nd 2026: Cathrine told us that she wants us to make a clean look with soft green colors that represent the planet, and rounded features. She also mentioned that she wants the main goal to be that it is easy for visitors to find information about the cats and the cafe. Furthermore, she also wants it to be easy for people to adopt cats by the website, and to get all the information that they need.

Purpose, goals and audience

Our purpose is to create a website that promotes the cat cafe and its values, while providing information about the cats, menu, and policies.

The goals are to increase awareness of the rescued cats, where they can find a loving home, or just feel comfortable and safe. Furthermore, we aim to educate visitors about the importance of adopting cats and supporting animal welfare.

Audience

  • Individuals: People who love cats and want to spend time with them. Many want to get to know the cats before they meet them at the cafe. The Cats page shows this.
  • Families and friends: Those who want to look at the menu and book a reservation before going to the cafe. The Menu and Booking pages show this.
  • People seeking stress relief and a peaceful atmosphere. We want a website that will provide a calming experience for our users. The website will explain how the environment can help reduce stress. The About page shows this.
  • Organizations and people: Those who are interested in animal welfare and supporting rescue organizations. Here you can find information about our partnerships and how to get involved. The Contact page shows this.
  • Pet adopters: they need to see each cat's age and personality, and know how to apply. The Cats page shows this, with a link to the adoption form.

Navigation structure

Our navigation structure is made with the intention of making the website easy to use. With links on every page back to the homepage through clicking on the logo, users can easily make it back to the beginning. The different pages of the website are linked at the top at all times so users can easily jump between. There are on some pages links to subpages like booking or adoption where they can fill out a booking/adoption form.

User journey

A visitor who wants to adopt a cat:

  1. The visitor opens the homepage.
  2. The visitor clicks "Cats" in the navigation bar.
  3. The visitor reads the cat profiles and finds a cat that is up for adoption.
  4. The visitor clicks the link to the adoption page and fills out the adoption form.

Common layout and look

Fonts

We use the font Nunito on the whole website, with sans-serif as a backup.

  • Body text: Nunito, 1rem, regular
  • Main heading (h1): Nunito, 2rem, medium 500
  • Section headings (h2): Nunito, 1.5rem, medium 500
  • Navigation links: Nunito, 1.2rem, regular (active page in semibold, 600)

Our client Cathrine Cat wished for rounded features. We felt that Nunito's rounded letterforms would complement this aesthetic.

Colors

  • Background: #FBF6EF (warm off-white)
  • Header and navigation bar: #FFFFFF (white)
  • Line under the header: #DCE3DC (light grey-green)
  • Main heading (h1): #6F8F78 (soft green)
  • Section headings (h2): #3A3A2C (dark grey)
  • Subheadings (h3): #4B4A42 (dark grey)
  • Body text: #4B4A42 (dark grey)
  • Links and menu button: #5A7462 (darker green), hover: #3A3A2C
  • Borders on content boxes: #6F8F78 (soft green)
  • Footer text: black

Our client Cathrine Cat wanted a clean look with soft colors that represent the planet. We therefore chose a calm palette of soft greens on a warm off-white background, with green used as an accent for headings, links and borders. To make the text easy to read, we use dark grey for the body text and a darker green for links, as the original soft green did not have enough contrast against the background.

Navigation bar, header and footer

Navigation bar: The navigation bar will be visible on all pages and will contain our 6 links: Home, About, Cats, Menu, Booking, and Contact. The active page is shown in semibold. On screens under 700px, the navigation bar will be replaced by a hamburger menu.

Header: The header lies at the top of the page and the color of the header is #FFFFFF (white). The header will contain the website's name and logo.

Footer: The footer lies at the bottom of every page. It shows the café's contact information (email and phone number) and links to Instagram and Facebook, so visitors can easily get in touch. At the very bottom, a small line created by footer.js shows the names of the developers.

Content of each page

Homepage - index.html

The homepage is the starting point for when someone opens the website. It is also the page that every other page on the website will link back to. The page will include pathways for all the other pages of the website. The page is very basic and has the other pages linked at the top to make them easy to find.

The homepage will include:

  1. Introduction to the café
  2. Basic information about the café
  3. Pictures of the café

By having the different pages of the website clearly linked at the top, this will make it easy for the user to understand how to get from the homepage to different parts of the website. The location and necessary information is on the homepage so that the users dont have to look far for the most important information.

About us - about.html

The about us page of the website is for those who want to know more about the owner and her goals. It includes a photo of the owner and a little about her and her motivation behind the café. It also includes some information about the cafe's goals. This page also includes the logo of the cafe that will be able to click and bring back to the homepage.

The about us page will include:

  1. A picture of the owner
  2. Information about the café's goals
  3. A link to the contact us page of the website

This section of the site will help the users understand the motivation behind the cafe. The contact us link will hopefully help the users find out how to contact the café and also urge them to continue exploring the website.

Our cats - cats.html

The our cats section is for those who are interested in the different cats they can visit and/or adopt. There are different profiles of all the cats at the cafe and a link to an adoption form they can fill out.

The our cats page will include:

  1. Profile cards of all the cats from the café
  2. Information about if they are up for adoption
  3. A link to a subpage with information about adoption and a form to fill out.

We chose to show each cat on its own card, with a picture, name, age and personality in the same place every time. This makes it easy for visitors to compare the cats and quickly find one that suits them. The cards are placed in a grid, which shows three cards per row on a computer and one per row on a phone.

Menu - menu.html

The menu section is a page where anyone can look at what the cafe has to serve. It shows all the drink items, food items and treats guests can buy to feed the cats.

The menu page will include:

  1. A food menu for humans
  2. A drink menu for humans
  3. A food and drink menu for the cats

The menu is divided into food, drinks and treats for the cats, so guests can quickly find what they are looking for. Families and friends can look at the menu before they visit, and plan what they want to order.

Contact us - contact.html

The contact us part of the website is a page where people van find any information they need to contact the cafe. They have normal forms of contact like email and phone number, but they can also view all the cafes social media platforms. There is also a link on the website for people who want to book a table and they will be sent to a booking form.

The contact us page will include:

  1. Links to the café's social medias
  2. The café's email and phone number
  3. A link to a subpage for booking a table at the café

All contact information is gathered on one page, so visitors do not have to search the website to get in touch. The link to the booking form is placed here because many visitors who contact the café want to book a table.

Minimum requirements

Different JavaScript-functions

We are having 4 different JavaScripts, nav.js which is the navigation bar, footer.js that shows our names in the bottom of every HTML file. A hover.js that enlarges the “adopt me” button. The last JavaScript file we will have is a validation for the booking.html, which checks if you have filled in the two fields.

Four distinct responsive components

  1. We have the navbar, when the screen-size is under 700px the navbar turns into a burger-menu.
  2. We are also planning on using grid to scale the cat-cards. For example: we have 3 on computer-screens, 2 on ipads and 1 on phones.
  3. The third component is that the pictures will change size to adjust to the screen size, this will also make the usability improve.
  4. The last component we are planning on is that the textboxes will change according to the layout. We can use Flexbox and flex-wrap to make this work.

How we are using CSS for the layout now

We use CSS grid for the header. The .meny class is a grid with three columns (grid-template-columns) and the .nav-grid class uses grid-template-columns: auto auto to lay out the navigation links.
The .info class uses Flexbox, display:flex with gap:2rem, so the author, client and date on the P1 HTML document line up, side by side.
We also use width, padding, margin and border to change the layout of content boxes. The textbox1 and textbox2-classes both use width:90%, max-width: 1500px and padding 20px so they dont get to wide for the window. We have also use border and border-radius to give a softer look. All layout is now written in one stylesheet, style.css.

What we plan to add

Cat cards – A CSS grid where the number of columns adapts to the screen width.
Booking form –Flexbox/Grid so the input boxes will stack on mobile. This is so the boxes won't be too narrow to write in on phones.
@media rules- to change the layout below 700px, like we have started doing with the burger-menu.

Accessibility measures

ARIA- Accessible Rich Internet Applications, it's a set of attributes you add to HTML to share extra information to the screen readers that necessarily isn't clear from just the code itself.
In the nav.js we have used aria-label that gives an element a name that the screen reader reads out.
aria-expanded tells us if something is open or closed. button aria-expanded="false/true"
aria-current=“page” marks the link to the page the user is on, <a href="booking.html" aria-current="page">Booking</a> here the screen reader will say “Booking, current page.”

Alt text on images – every <img> has an alt that describes the image, for example: alt=“Toffee, a Scottish fold cat”

Labels- labels are what points to the id of the input. For example in the booking.html, the <label for="email">Email</label> will make the screen reader read “Email”

Visible focus for keyboard users- we will use CSS to make an outline when you navigate with Tab through the website.

Contrast between text and background – we have made the font color a color that will pass the WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) requirements.
