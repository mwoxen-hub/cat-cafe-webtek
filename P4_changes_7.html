1. Changes from the design

Page What changed Why
index.html Sections shown as white cards, and the map scales to the screen width. The cards separate the content into clear blocks, and the fixed-width map overflowed on mobile
cats.html / cat-filter.js Added filter buttons that show all cats, only cats available for adoption, or only café residents. The selected button is highlighted. Visitors who want to adopt can quickly find the cats that are available, without reading through every cat on the page.
menu.html Added category filter buttons. Easier to find items on a long menu
booking.html The booking form asks for name, email, date, time and number of guests (1–8). All fields are required. limits the choices to what the café can handle, and the required fields make sure we have the information needed to confirm a booking.
booking.js Blocks past dates, limits the time to opening hours (different on weekdays and weekends), and shows a confirmation when the form is submitted. Visitors can't book a table in the past or outside opening hours, and they get immediate feedback that the booking went through.
contact.html The contact information, social media, booking, adoption and job application links are shown as five boxes side by side that wrap on small screens. Visitors find every way to reach us or take the next step on one page, and the boxes adapt to the screen size. The job box was added to meet our requirement from P1 that visitors can find job opportunities.
adoption.html Besides the application form, the page explains why to adopt, how the process works and what it costs. The form has required fields, a phone-number format, an age limit and a choice of cat. Visitors can see the whole process and the fees before applying, and the form checks the input so we get complete, correctly formatted applications.
adoption.js Shows the value of the "previous pets" slider next to it as it moves, and shows a confirmation message when the form is submitted. The slider has no visible number on its own, so visitors can't tell what value they chose. The confirmation tells them that the application was received.
about.html The owner is shown with a photo next to the text, the goals are shown as four cards in a grid, a "Support our cause" section explains our donations to WWF, and there is a link to the contact page at the bottom. The layout makes the page easy to scan, the donation section lets visitors know how they can support animals, and the contact link gives them a clear next step.
footer.js Footer is built with JavaScript, and the email and phone number are clickable links. The same footer appears on every page from one file, and visitors can write or call straight from the footer, especially on mobile.
nav.js / style.css The burger menu now appears at 900px instead of 700px On tablet-sized screens the six links were cramped, so the menu collapses earlier.
filter.js New script that filters the menu by category and highlights the selected button. The menu has five categories, so visitors can jump straight to what they want, such as only drinks or only treats, without scrolling.

4. Modularity

Elements that are repeated on every page are written once in shared files, so no code is duplicated between pages:

  • nav.js builds the header, navigation and burger menu on every page (Lea).
  • footer.js builds the footer on every page (Lea).
  • style.css is the one stylesheet for all pages (Marie).
  • adoption.js is loaded only on the adoption page (Elena).
  • filter.js, cat-filter.js, booking.js and adoption.js are used only on the pages that need them.
